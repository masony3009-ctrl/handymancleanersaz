Room by Room

The Airbnb Turnover Checklist

First 10 Minutes

  • Walk the house and note which beds were used
  • Photograph damage or missing items and report them to the host
  • Strip used beds and collect every towel
  • Start laundry and the dishwasher first, so they finish while you clean

Laundry

  • Wash sheets and towels in separate loads; bleach stained whites
  • Dry everything fully, with nothing left wet in the washer

Bathrooms (each one)

  • Vacuum hair from the floor, toilet, sink, and shower; shake out the bath mat
  • Bleach the toilet and wipe it top to bottom with disposable towels
  • Clean the sink, shower, glass doors, and mirror; remove soap scum
  • Restock toilet paper and spares, shampoo, conditioner, body wash, hand soap, and a hand towel
  • Vacuum and mop the floor

Bedrooms

  • Make every used bed with fresh linens: fitted sheet, top sheet, comforter, pillows, decor
  • Dust furniture and vacuum floors

Kitchen

  • Wipe counters, table, and stove: no crumbs or dried food
  • Wipe the microwave, check the oven and clean its door glass, empty the toaster tray
  • Clear guest leftovers from the fridge, wipe shelves, empty the freezer
  • Empty the coffee maker; wipe cabinet and appliance fronts
  • Wash and put away dishes; rinse the sink
  • Restock dish soap, hand soap, paper towels, and a dish towel
  • Take out the trash, put in a new liner, unplug small appliances

Living Areas

  • Dust furniture, arrange couch pillows, vacuum rugs

Floors & Outside

  • Vacuum and mop the whole house, last
  • Check floors for sticky spots
  • Patio: pick up trash and put furniture back

Closing Out

  • Set out fresh towels to the house count
  • Do a room-by-room final walkthrough
  • Take the trash out and lock the doors
  • Send photo proof to the host and flag anything that is off

Host Tips

Make Every Turnover Faster

  • Keep two sets of linens per bed. The clean set goes on while the used set washes, so rooms are ready without waiting on the dryer.
  • Write your house count down. List how many towels, pillows, and toiletries each stay gets, so every turnover sets out the same amount.
  • Stock a locked supply closet. Spare toilet paper, soaps, coffee, and trash bags on site mean your cleaner can restock without a store run.
  • Share your booking calendar. A calendar link turns every checkout into a scheduled clean, including back-to-back stays and changed dates.
  • Ask for proof every time. A walkthrough video after each clean gives you time-stamped documentation of the home's condition if you ever need to file a damage claim.
