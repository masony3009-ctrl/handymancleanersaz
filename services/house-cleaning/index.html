Routine Clean

Keep the Home You Already Like

From $150 per visit

This is the standing visit — weekly, biweekly, or monthly. The home is already in reasonable shape and the job is keeping it there, so we work the same room-by-room checklist every time and you stop thinking about it.

  • Kitchen counters, sink, cooktop, and appliance exteriors
  • Bathrooms scrubbed and disinfected — toilets, showers, tubs, vanities
  • All floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Dusting: surfaces, furniture, ceiling fans, fixtures
  • Mirrors and interior glass left streak-free
  • High-touch points wiped — switches, handles, pulls
  • Trash emptied and taken out

Best for: a home that gets a proper clean already and needs it maintained. Not for: a first visit after a long gap — start with a deep clean, then this keeps it there.

Deep Clean

Everything a Routine Clean Skips

From $280

A deep clean is the reset. It covers the whole routine checklist and then keeps going into the places that only get touched a couple of times a year — which is where the grime that bothers you actually lives.

  • Everything in a routine clean, plus:
  • Inside the oven and inside the refrigerator
  • Inside cabinets and drawers
  • Baseboards hand-wiped, floor edges detailed
  • Door frames, door tops, and switch plates
  • Window sills, tracks, and blinds
  • Shower glass descaled and grout scrubbed
  • Behind and under furniture we can safely move
  • Cobwebs, vents, and light fixtures

Best for: your first visit with us, a home that hasn't had a thorough clean in six months or more, or a seasonal reset before guests.

Move-Out Clean

Walk Into the Inspection Confident

$300–$400

When you hand back the keys, the landlord inspects the home and itemizes anything they think needs cleaning — straight out of your deposit. This is a deep clean on an empty home, done to the standard that inspection applies.

  • Everything in a deep clean, on an empty home
  • Inside the oven, fridge, cabinets, and drawers — what gets checked first
  • Baseboards, switch plates, door frames, and interior windows
  • Laundry and utility areas

Best for: end of lease, selling, or taking possession of a home someone else just left. Worth knowing: the photo set is the part tenants tell us mattered — it's dated evidence of how the home was left.

House Cleaning FAQ

How much does a regular house cleaning cost?

Routine cleans start at $150 per visit. The exact number depends on the size of the home and how much there is to do — we walk the home first, for free, and give you a flat per-visit price. That number is locked before we start, and it doesn't change unless you ask us to add something.

What's the difference between a routine clean, a deep clean, and a move-out clean?

A routine clean keeps an already-tidy home that way — kitchen, bathrooms, floors, dusting, high-touch points — on a weekly, biweekly, or monthly visit, from $150. A deep clean adds everything routine skips: inside the oven and fridge, inside cabinets and drawers, baseboards, door frames, switch plates, window tracks, and scrubbed grout, from $280. A move-out clean is a deep clean on an empty home, done to the standard a landlord inspects, and runs $300 to $400.

How much does a move-out cleaning cost?

Most move-out cleans run $300 to $400 depending on the size and condition of the home. We do a free walkthrough first and give you an exact flat quote — the number we quote is the number you pay, locked before we start. No mid-job surprises.

Do I need a deep clean before starting regular cleanings?

Usually, yes — and we'll tell you honestly at the walkthrough if you don't. If a home hasn't had a thorough clean in a while, the first visit is a deep clean so everything starts from the same baseline. After that, routine visits keep it there at the lower routine price.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No — most of our clients aren't. We confirm access ahead of time, walk the property before we start, and send you photo proof when we're done, so you can see the finished home from anywhere. You'll always know exactly who was in your home: the same two people, every time.

Do you clean inside the fridge and oven?

Yes on deep cleans and move-out cleans — interior appliance cleaning is standard on both, and it's what a landlord looks at first. On routine cleans it's an easy add-on: just empty the fridge beforehand and we'll handle the rest.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes, we bring all of our own equipment and supplies. If you prefer we use specific products you provide — for delicate surfaces or allergies — just tell us at the walkthrough.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry $1 million in general liability coverage. If your landlord, property manager, or HOA needs documentation before we start, we can provide a certificate of insurance — and name them as an additional insured if their agreement requires it.

Can you do a last-minute or same-day cleaning?

Often, yes. Same-day and last-minute cleans are a normal part of our week when the schedule allows. Text us at 480-800-7789 during business hours and you'll get a straight yes or no fast — usually within minutes.
