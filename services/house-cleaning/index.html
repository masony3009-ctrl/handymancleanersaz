Our Written Standard

The House Cleaning Checklist We Work From

Every clean runs against a written scope — you see the checklist, we mark it complete, and the finished home gets photographed. This is that checklist, condensed.

Whole Home

  • Cobwebs removed from ceilings and corners
  • Ceiling fans, fixtures, and vents dusted
  • Doors, frames, handles, and trim wiped
  • Interior glass and mirrors streak-free

Kitchen

  • Counters cleaned and sanitized
  • Backsplash and cooktop wall degreased
  • Sink, faucet, and drain area scrubbed
  • Cabinet fronts, pulls, and toe-kicks wiped
  • Stovetop, grates, and knobs degreased
  • Appliance exteriors polished

Bathrooms

  • Toilets cleaned and disinfected, base to tank
  • Showers and tubs scrubbed, glass de-spotted
  • Vanity, sink, and faucets polished
  • Mirrors and light fixtures cleaned
  • Floors disinfected corner to corner

Bedrooms & Living Areas

  • Furniture and surfaces dusted
  • Carpet vacuumed, edges and under light furniture
  • Hard floors vacuumed and damp-mopped
  • Closet shelves and rods dusted
  • High-touch points wiped down

Final Walkthrough

  • Room-by-room quality check against the scope
  • Trash bagged and placed at the agreed spot
  • Photo proof of the finished home sent to you
  • Anything we couldn't complete, flagged honestly

Questions

House Cleaning FAQ

How much does a regular house cleaning cost?

Routine cleans start at $150 per visit. The exact number depends on the size of the home and how much there is to do — we walk the home first, for free, and give you a flat per-visit price. That number is locked before we start, and it doesn't change unless you ask us to add something.

What's included in a routine house cleaning?

Kitchen counters, sink, cooktop, and appliance exteriors; bathrooms scrubbed and disinfected; all floors vacuumed and mopped; surfaces, furniture, ceiling fans, and fixtures dusted; mirrors and interior glass left streak-free; high-touch points wiped; and trash taken out. We work the same written checklist every visit, weekly, biweekly, or monthly, from $150.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No — most of our clients aren't. We confirm access ahead of time, walk the property before we start, and send you photo proof when we're done, so you can see the finished home from anywhere.

Do you clean inside the fridge and oven?

Yes, as an easy add-on to a routine clean: just empty the fridge beforehand and we'll handle the rest.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes, we bring all of our own equipment and supplies. If you prefer we use specific products you provide — for delicate surfaces or allergies — just tell us at the walkthrough.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry $1 million in general liability coverage. If your landlord, property manager, or HOA needs documentation before we start, we can provide a certificate of insurance — and name them as an additional insured if their agreement requires it.

Can you do a last-minute or same-day cleaning?

Often, yes. Same-day and last-minute cleans are a normal part of our week when the schedule allows. Text us at 480-800-7789 during business hours and you'll get a straight yes or no fast — usually within minutes.

What areas do you serve for house cleaning?

Queen Creek, San Tan Valley, Gilbert, and Mesa, Arizona. We're based in San Tan Valley, so most homes in the Southeast Valley are a short drive — which is what makes same-day visits possible. See our service areas →
