Our Written Standard

The House Cleaning Checklist We Work From

Every clean runs against a written scope — you see the checklist, we mark it complete, and the finished home gets photographed. This is that checklist, condensed.

Whole Home

  • Cobwebs removed from ceilings and corners
  • Ceiling fans, fixtures, and vents dusted
  • Doors, frames, handles, and trim wiped
  • Baseboards wiped, floor edges detailed
  • Switch plates and wall controls cleaned
  • Interior glass and mirrors streak-free

Kitchen

  • Counters cleaned and sanitized
  • Backsplash and cooktop wall degreased
  • Sink, faucet, and drain area scrubbed
  • Cabinet fronts, pulls, and toe-kicks wiped
  • Stovetop, grates, and knobs degreased
  • Appliance exteriors polished

Bathrooms

  • Toilets cleaned and disinfected, base to tank
  • Showers and tubs scrubbed, glass de-spotted
  • Vanity, sink, and faucets polished
  • Mirrors and light fixtures cleaned
  • Floors disinfected corner to corner

Bedrooms & Living Areas

  • Furniture and surfaces dusted
  • Carpet vacuumed, edges and under light furniture
  • Hard floors vacuumed and damp-mopped
  • Closet shelves and rods dusted
  • High-touch points wiped down

Move-Out Extras

  • Inside the oven and refrigerator
  • Inside cabinets and drawers
  • Blinds, sills, and window tracks
  • Laundry and utility areas
  • Garage sweep-out — just ask at the walkthrough

Final Walkthrough

  • Room-by-room quality check against the scope
  • Trash bagged and placed at the agreed spot
  • Photo proof of the finished home sent to you
  • Anything we couldn't complete, flagged honestly

House Cleaning FAQ

How much does a move-out cleaning cost?

Most move-out cleans run $300 to $400 depending on the size and condition of the home. We do a free walkthrough first and give you an exact flat quote — the number we quote is the number you pay, locked before we start. No mid-job surprises.

What's included in a house cleaning?

Every clean follows a written room-by-room checklist: kitchens degreased and sanitized, bathrooms scrubbed and disinfected, floors vacuumed and mopped, baseboards and ceiling fans dusted, mirrors and interior glass cleaned, and trash removed. You get photo proof when we finish.

What's the difference between a move-out clean and a deep clean?

A move-out clean assumes the home is empty and covers what landlords actually inspect: inside the oven, fridge, cabinets, and drawers, plus baseboards, switch plates, and door frames. A deep clean covers an occupied home at the same level of detail while working around your belongings.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No — most of our clients aren't. We confirm access ahead of time, walk the property before we start, and send you photo proof when we're done, so you can see the finished home from anywhere. You'll always know exactly who was in your home: the same two people, every time.

Do you clean inside the fridge and oven?

Yes. Interior appliance cleaning is standard on every move-out clean — that's what the landlord looks at first. On recurring house cleans it's an easy add-on: just empty the fridge beforehand and we'll handle the rest.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies?

Yes, we bring all of our own equipment and supplies. If you prefer we use specific products you provide — for delicate surfaces or allergies — just tell us at the walkthrough.

Can you do a last-minute or same-day cleaning?

Often, yes. Same-day and last-minute cleans are a normal part of our week when the schedule allows. Text us at 480-800-7789 during business hours and you'll get a straight yes or no fast — usually within minutes.

Queen Creek, San Tan Valley, and Gilbert, Arizona. We're based in San Tan Valley, so most homes in the Southeast Valley are a short drive — which is what makes same-day visits possible. See our service areas →
