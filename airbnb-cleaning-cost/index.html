What Changes the Price

  • Home size. Bedrooms, bathrooms, and how many beds need fresh linens are the biggest factor. A larger home takes more laundry and more time.
  • Holidays. Holiday cleans add $50. Weekends cost the same as weekdays.
  • Repairs. If something needs fixing between guests, our handyman rate is $100 for the first hour and $80 for each hour after. Not a licensed contractor.
  • Guest supplies. Toilet paper, soaps, and coffee are not part of the cleaning price. Ship them to the property or fund restock runs.

What does not change the price: same-day turnovers, back-to-back bookings, and weekend checkouts. A same-day turnover is the same flat rate, with no rush fee.

For Hosts

Setting Your Airbnb Cleaning Fee

Airbnb lets you add a cleaning fee to each booking, and guests see it in the total price before they book. Many hosts set that fee close to what their cleaner charges per turnover, so every stay pays for its own reset. With a flat rate per home, the math stays simple: you know exactly what each turnover costs before you set the fee.

Pricing FAQ

Airbnb Cleaning Cost Questions

Is a same-day turnover more expensive?

No. Same-day turnovers are the same flat rate, with no rush fee. Request by 9 AM that morning when you can.

Is laundry included?

Yes. We strip the used beds, wash the sheets and towels on-site, and remake the beds during the same visit. Keeping a second set of linens per bed lets us remake rooms while laundry runs.

Do you bring cleaning supplies?

Yes. We bring our own cleaning equipment and supplies. Guest consumables like toilet paper, soaps, and coffee are separate: you ship them to the property or fund restock runs, and we tell you when anything is running low.

Do weekends cost more?

No. We work weekends at the same rate. Holiday cleans add $50.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before the clean. Inside 24 hours there is a $75 fee.

How do I pay?

You pay after each clean, by Zelle, Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Turno, or invoice.
